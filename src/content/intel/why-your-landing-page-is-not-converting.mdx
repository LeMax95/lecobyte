## 01_The_Common_Assumption

When a landing page does not convert, people assume:

> “The design is bad.”

In most cases, that’s wrong.

---

## 02_The_Real_Problem

What actually breaks:

- forms not sending properly  
- leads not reaching CRM  
- no tracking of traffic source  
- slow or unstable setup  
- broken mobile flow  

---

## 03_The_Invisible_Failure

The worst scenario is this:

> Leads exist — but you don’t see them.

Or worse:

> You see leads — but don’t know where they came from.

---

## 04_Why_Design_Is_Overrated

Design matters.

But only AFTER:

- tracking works  
- forms work  
- CRM works  

Without that:

Design is just decoration.

---

## 05_The_System_You_Actually_Need

A working landing setup includes:

- page  
- tracking  
- attribution  
- CRM integration  
- routing logic  

Remove one — the system breaks.

---

## 06_Final_Truth

If your landing page “doesn’t convert”:

It might be converting.

You just don’t see it.
