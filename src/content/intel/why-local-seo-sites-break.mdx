## 01_The_Myth

Most people think:

> “We just need more content.”

That is rarely the issue.

---

## 02_The_Real_Problem

Local sites fail because:

- pages are inconsistent  
- structure is random  
- services are unclear  
- no scalable system exists  

---

## 03_Service_Area_Chaos

Typical scenario:

- 10 city pages  
- all slightly different  
- no clear pattern  

Google sees:

→ inconsistency  
→ weak signals  

---

## 04_The_System_Missing

What works instead:

- structured pages  
- repeatable patterns  
- consistent hierarchy  
- controlled variation
