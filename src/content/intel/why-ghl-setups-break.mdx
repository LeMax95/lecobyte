## 01_The_Blame_Game

When something breaks, people say:

> “GHL is buggy.”

Most of the time — it isn’t.

---

## 02_Where_It_Actually_Breaks

- webhooks not consistent  
- tags missing  
- forms disconnected  
- attribution lost  
- workflows unclear  

---

## 03_The_Real_Issue

GHL is not the system.

It is just one part of it.

---

## 04_What_Is_Missing

You need:

- landing page  
- tracking  
- attribution  
- routing logic  
- CRM  

Not just GHL.

---

## 05_Final

If your setup feels fragile:

It’s not the tool.

It’s the system around it.
